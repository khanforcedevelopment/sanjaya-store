Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and protect your personal data.

Effective date: 9 October 2026
Privacy PolicyRefund & Returns PolicyTerms & Conditions

1. Who we are

This website is operated by Sanjaya Trading FZE (“we”, “us”, “our”), a company registered in the Ajman Free Zone, United Arab Emirates. We are responsible for the personal data collected through this website.

We handle personal data in line with the UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 on the Protection of Personal Data (PDPL) and other applicable UAE laws.

2. Personal data we collect

  • Contact form: your name, company name, email address, phone number and message.
  • Quote requests on WhatsApp: your WhatsApp number, name and the products and quantities you request.
  • Orders and deliveries: delivery address, contact person and invoicing details you give us.
  • Technical data: basic information such as IP address, browser type and pages visited, collected automatically by our hosting provider for security and performance.
  • Online orders: your name, phone, email, delivery address and the products you buy.

Card payments are processed by our payment provider (Stripe) on its secure payment page. We never see or store your full card number. We receive only confirmation of the payment and limited details such as the last four digits and card type.

3. Why we use your data

  • To reply to your enquiries and send you quotations.
  • To process, deliver and invoice your orders.
  • To keep records required by UAE law (for example, accounting and VAT records).
  • To keep the website secure and working properly.
  • To send you offers or price lists, only if you agree. You can opt out at any time.

We process your data on the basis of your consent, to take steps before or under a contract with you, to meet our legal obligations, and for our legitimate business interests.

4. Who we share data with

We do not sell your personal data. We only share it when needed with:

  • Service providers that help us run the website and our business, such as our payment provider (Stripe), our website host (Netlify, which processes contact-form submissions), WhatsApp (Meta) for messaging, and Google Fonts for website fonts.
  • Delivery and logistics partners, to deliver your order.
  • Authorities, when required by UAE law or a valid legal request.

Some of these providers may store data outside the UAE. When this happens, we rely on providers that apply appropriate safeguards, as required by the PDPL.

5. Cookies and local storage

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. Your shopping cart is saved only in your own browser (local storage) so it is not lost when you refresh the page. Our payment provider may use essential cookies on its payment page for security and fraud prevention.

6. How long we keep data

We keep enquiry data for up to 2 years after our last contact, and order and invoice records for as long as UAE law requires (usually at least 5 years). After that, we delete or anonymise it.

7. Your rights

Under the PDPL you may have the right to:

  • ask for a copy of the personal data we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct or complete inaccurate data;
  • ask us to delete your data or restrict how we use it;
  • object to processing, including for marketing;
  • withdraw your consent at any time.

To use any of these rights, contact us using the details below. We will reply within 30 days.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data, including encrypted (HTTPS) connections. No online service is 100% secure, but we work to protect your information and will act quickly if a problem occurs.

9. Children

This website is intended for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page with its effective date.
