Refund & Returns Policy

Our commitment to fresh, quality produce.

Effective date: 9 October 2026
Privacy PolicyRefund & Returns PolicyTerms & Conditions

1. Overview

Sanjaya Trading FZE supplies fresh fruits and vegetables. Because our products are fresh and perishable, returns work differently from other goods. This policy explains how we handle quality issues, returns and refunds, in line with the UAE Federal Law No. 15 of 2020 on Consumer Protection and its Executive Regulations.

Nothing in this policy limits any rights you have under UAE law.

2. Check your order on delivery

Please check your products at the time of delivery, in front of our driver where possible. If anything is damaged, spoiled, missing or not what you ordered, you may refuse those items at delivery.

3. Reporting a problem

If you find a problem after delivery, please contact us within 24 hours of receiving your order on WhatsApp +971 56 911 0077 or by email admin@sanjayatrading.com with:

  • your name and order / invoice number;
  • the product(s) and quantity affected;
  • clear photos of the problem and the packaging.

Please keep the affected products until we have reviewed your claim, as we may need to collect or inspect them.

4. When you are eligible

We will offer a replacement, credit note or refund if a product:

  • arrives damaged, spoiled or not fresh;
  • is different from what you ordered (wrong product, variety or grade);
  • is short in quantity or weight compared with your invoice.

5. What is not eligible

  • Products that were correct and in good condition at delivery but were later stored or handled improperly.
  • Normal natural differences in size, shape or colour of fresh produce.
  • Change of mind, once fresh products have been delivered.
  • Claims reported after 24 hours, unless the problem could not reasonably have been noticed earlier.
  • Special or custom orders (for example, specially sourced or imported items), unless the products were faulty or did not match the agreed specification.

6. Cancellations

  • You may cancel an order free of charge before it is confirmed or dispatched.
  • Once fresh products are packed or dispatched, cancellation may not be possible, or the costs already incurred may be charged.
  • For wholesale, import or export orders, the cancellation terms in your quotation or contract apply.

7. How refunds are made

Once your claim is approved, we will provide a replacement, a credit note for your next order, or a refund, as agreed with you. Refunds are made using the original payment method (or by bank transfer for cash payments) and are normally processed within 7–14 working days. Your bank may take extra time to show the amount.
